Your Data
Privacy
policy.

We keep this simple: we don't collect, store, or sell your personal information. Here's exactly what happens with your data.

  1. What we collect.

    When you place an order, you voluntarily enter your name, email address, phone number, and shipping address into the cart form on our website. This information is used solely to pre-fill the Venmo payment note so we know where to ship your kit and how to reach you. We do not store this information anywhere — it exists only in your browser until you close the page or complete your order.

  2. How payment works.

    All payments are processed through Venmo, operated by PayPal, Inc. When you tap "Pay via Venmo," you are taken to Venmo's app or website to complete the transaction. Any financial data (card numbers, bank details) is handled entirely by Venmo — we never see or receive it. Venmo's own privacy policy governs that interaction.

  3. What Venmo shares with us.

    After a completed Venmo payment, we receive your Venmo username, the payment amount, and the note you sent (which includes the shipping details you entered). This is the only personal information we have on record for your order, and it is used only to fulfill and ship your kit.

  4. We don't use cookies or tracking.

    This website does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or first-party cookies. We do load Bootstrap CSS and Google Fonts from third-party CDNs, which may log your IP address as part of serving those files — this is standard practice and is governed by their respective privacy policies.

  5. We don't sell or share your data.

    Your name, email, phone number, and shipping address are used only to process and ship your order. We will never sell, rent, or share this information with any third party for marketing or any other purpose.

  6. How we use your contact info.

    We may use your email or phone number to follow up about your order — for example, to confirm shipment, provide a tracking number, or respond to a return request. We will not send you unsolicited marketing messages.

  7. Data retention.

    Order information (name, address, Venmo note) is retained only as long as needed to fulfill your order, handle any returns, and meet basic record-keeping needs. We do not maintain a customer database or mailing list.

  8. Your rights.

    You can request that we delete any personal information we hold about you at any time. Just reach out at tbengels@gmail.com and we'll take care of it promptly.

  9. Contact.

    Questions about this privacy policy? Email us at tbengels@gmail.com or text 651-395-1906.
