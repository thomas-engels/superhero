FAQ
Questions.
Do I need training? +
You don't need to be an EMT, but a basic first aid and CPR class is strongly recommended. Each kit includes the field guide and access to a companion emergency guidance app. For tool-specific usage, refer to the manufacturer's instructions included with each item.
Can you customize it? +
While I recommend the Superhero kit for every activity (even while driving your car), I can work with you to create the perfect kit for your specific needs.
How many people can one kit help? +
I strongly recommend one kit per person. Multiple kits eliminate the "single point of failure" risk (e.g., someone forgets the first aid kit or drops it in a river), increase speed of treatment when your group is spread out, and can be combined for severe injuries — some injuries require multiple tourniquets to stop a bleed.

For group trips, one kit per person is the gold standard — some injuries require multiple tourniquets to stop a bleed. Scale as needed.
How does it attach? +
Designed to mount to a climbing harness, running vest, or backpack via a combination of carabiners, cords, and webbing.
Can I see the full contents list? +
Yes — text or email directly to get the complete contents list. (651) 395-1906 or tbengels@gmail.com.
What about comfort items like tweezers or SAM splints? +
Both kits focus on critical interventions and stay ultralight. The Superhero Professional adds hemostatic gauze for junctional wound packing — an advanced skill best used by those with formal training. For comfort items specific to longer trips (splints, blister care, etc.), reach out to discuss a custom kit.

The Person Behind It
Built by someone
who's been there.

This isn't a brand — it's a personal project born from an obsession with being prepared in the outdoors. The activities I love — climbing, skiing, biking, scuba diving, surfing — are inherently dangerous, and I've seen firsthand what poor judgment and mediocre first aid can cost. Being prepared is what's let me keep enjoying them safely. I want others to have that same edge. Owning one of my first aid kits AND educating yourself on how to use these tools means you're prepared for the moments that matter most.

TE
Thomas Engels
Founder · Software Engineer · EMT

A senior software engineer at a Fortune 100 company with 6 years of professional experience. However, my true passion lies in pushing my limits outdoors as a climber/runner/skier with exceptional friends and family. Risking life/limb and outdoor limit exploration are inextricably linked. Out of necessity and curiosity, I quietly expanded my wilderness medicine in parallel with my engineering career (WFA in 2017, OECT for Ski Patrol in 2020, WFR in 2025, EMT-B in 2026; roughly 300 hours of formal training) and had the privilege of applying this knowledge on real patients as a volunteer ski patroller on the weekends and evenings in Minnesota and Colorado.

Through my wilderness medicine education and real patient treatment, I realized that generic first aid kits are overly focused on "comfort and quality of life" like treating blisters, small scrapes and bruises. While worthy features of a well-rounded first aid kit, the most important capability of a first aid kit should be keeping you alive long enough to transport you to definitive healthcare. It only takes 3 minutes for arterial/severe bleeding to kill you, and amazingly you cannot find a first aid kit at your local outdoor store that can control arterial bleeding; let alone control the bleeding for an extensive 8-10 hour SAR helicopter rescue in mountainous terrain.

I've been carrying DIY, custom first aid kits for real emergencies for the last 7 years (real story — this kit was on hand when a free-soloist fell 60 ft in Eldorado Canyon, Colorado). The Superhero First Aid Kit equips anyone to respond when serious emergencies happen — to be a superhero in the moment. A companion emergency guidance app and manufacturer training resources will give you what you need to use these tools confidently. No outcome can be guaranteed — but having the right tools, right mindset, and right training is what allows you to act when it matters.
